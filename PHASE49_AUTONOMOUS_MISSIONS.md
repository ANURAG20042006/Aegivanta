# Phase 49: Autonomous Cyber Defense Missions

## Overview
Autonomous Defense Missions provide goal-directed orchestration for complex threat containment operations without continuous human micro-management.

## Governance & Safety Boundaries
1. **Blast Radius Limits**: Every mission specifies a financial cap (e.g. $50,000 USD) representing the maximum allowable business disruption cost.
2. **Human Veto Windows**: For high/critical tier threats, autonomous actions are scheduled with a configurable countdown window (e.g., 30–60 seconds) during which SOC analysts can veto or modify the action.
3. **Auditability**: All actions taken during the mission are signed and immutably recorded.
