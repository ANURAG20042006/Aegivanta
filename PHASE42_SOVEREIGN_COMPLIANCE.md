# PHASE 42 — SOVEREIGN COMPLIANCE SPECIFICATION

## 1. Regulatory Alignments

- **GDPR (European Union)**: Ensures telemetry stays within approved EEA zones (`EU_WEST_1`, `EU_CENTRAL_1`).
- **FedRAMP / ITAR (United States)**: Enforces dedicated US GovCloud partitions.
- **APPI (Japan) & MAS (Singapore)**: Ensures APAC financial isolation.
