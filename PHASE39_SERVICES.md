# PHASE 39 — PREDICTIVE INTELLIGENCE & FORECASTING SERVICES

## 1. Services Overview

| Service Name | Path | Purpose |
|--------------|------|---------|
| `PredictiveForecastingService` | `backend/app/services/predictive_forecasting_service.py` | Models probabilistic attack vectors, asset categories, 30/60/90d horizons, and global horizon indicators. |
| `AdversarialSimulationService` | `backend/app/services/adversarial_simulation_service.py` | Simulates multi-stage attack escalation pathways, blast radius node counts, and mitigation directives. |
| `PredictivePostureService` | `backend/app/services/predictive_posture_service.py` | Evaluates consolidated predictive intelligence posture scorecard (0–100) and actionable priorities. |
