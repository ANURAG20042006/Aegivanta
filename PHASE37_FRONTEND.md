# PHASE 37 — FRONTEND AI SOC COMMAND CENTER

## 1. UI Tabs

`AISOCCommandCenter.tsx` provides 6 interactive tabs:
1. **AI SOC Overview**: Autonomy score, mean triage/response times, active cases, insider threats count, and priority directives.
2. **Autonomous Investigations**: Case cards detailing AI hypotheses, confidence scores, gathered evidence, and one-click action review.
3. **UEBA Profiles (URS)**: Table of monitored identities with peer group classifications, baseline metrics, and risk tier badges.
4. **Insider Threat Matrix**: Threat indicators with magnitude scores and forensic evidence summaries.
5. **Decision Traces & Audits**: Immutable ledger of AI recommendations, reasoning traces, and approving analyst signatures.
6. **Investigation Sandbox**: Real-time simulation interface for triggering deep autonomous AI triage on ingested alerts.
