# PHASE 34 — REMEDIATION CAMPAIGN BURN-DOWN SPECIFICATION

## 1. Remediation Campaigns

Remediation campaigns group related vulnerable assets under a unified remediation sprint:
- **Campaign Burn-Down Percentage**: $\frac{Assets_{Fixed}}{Assets_{Total}} \times 100\%$
- **Owner Accountability**: Assigns direct responsibility to SecOps, Infrastructure, or DevOps teams with target completion milestones.
