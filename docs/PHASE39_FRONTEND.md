# PHASE 39 — FRONTEND PREDICTIVE INTELLIGENCE COMMAND CENTER

## 1. UI Tabs

`PredictiveIntelCenter.tsx` delivers 6 interactive enterprise tabs:
1. **Predictive Intelligence Overview**: Predictive score, active forecast count, adversarial simulations, global horizon surge indicators, and mean probability metrics.
2. **Threat Forecasts (30/60/90d)**: Filterable catalog of emerging attack vectors with probability percentages, impact severities, and evidence rationales.
3. **Adversarial Blast Radius Sims**: Visual simulation cards with initial access vectors, full escalation pathways, blast node counts, and mitigation directives.
4. **Global Threat Horizon**: Trajectory trend cards tracking surging/stable threat categories and observed telemetry sightings.
5. **Probabilistic Projections**: 30-day and 90-day exposure horizon cards detailing confidence intervals and EPSS percentiles.
6. **Forecast Synthesizer**: Form to generate and predict emerging threat scenarios with custom target assets and time horizons.
