# Phase 47: Cyber ROI & Economic Risk Quantification Engine

## Overview
The Cyber ROI Engine provides mathematically rigorous economic quantification of cybersecurity defenses for C-suite and Board members.

## Economic Value Drivers
1. **Breach Cost Avoidance**: Quantifies expected financial loss prevented based on annualized loss expectancy (ALE) models and MITRE ATT&CK kill-chain disruption.
2. **Cyber Insurance Premium Optimization**: Leverages continuous attestation of controls (MFA, microsegmentation, EDR, autonomous containment) to negotiate premium discounts with underwriters ($145K average annual savings).
3. **Labor Cost Optimization**: Quantifies analyst labor saved by autonomous triage, correlation, and response ($520K annual savings).
4. **Gordon-Loeb Security Investment Optimization**: Validates that security expenditure sits within the optimal range of expected losses.
