# PHASE 33 — MITRE ENGAGE MATRIX ALIGNMENT SPECIFICATION

## 1. The 6 MITRE Engage Goals

1. **Expose**: Force adversary to reveal presence through canary triggers and honey SPN querying.
2. **Lure**: Entice attackers into controlled decoy environments using breadcrumbs.
3. **Redirect**: Steer attacker traffic away from legitimate enterprise assets to honeypots.
4. **Elicit**: Provoke adversary into executing specific tools, malware, or TTPs.
5. **Degrade**: Slow down and exhaust adversary resources.
6. **Disrupt**: Immediately terminate and contain attacker infrastructure.
