# Aegivanta — Phase 17: AI-Assisted Incident Triage & Explainability

## 1. Triage Safety Principles
- **No Fabricated Evidence**: AI synthesizes exclusively grounded alert attributes, network flows, and IOCs.
- **Explainable Reasoning**: Human-readable explanation and factor breakdown for every proposed action.
- **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop Gating**: Actions involving critical infrastructure require human administrator approval.
- **Recursive Secret Redaction**: Eliminates inadvertent credential exposure.
