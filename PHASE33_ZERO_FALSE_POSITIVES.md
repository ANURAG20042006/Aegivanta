# PHASE 33 — ZERO-FALSE-POSITIVE DECEPTION ARCHITECTURE SPECIFICATION

## 1. Zero False-Positive Principle

Traditional security controls suffer from high false-positive rates due to dual-use software and legitimate user anomalies. Deception technology guarantees a **100% True-Positive Rate**:
- Honeytokens have no legitimate business role.
- Honeypot decoys have no legitimate users.
- Any interaction with a decoy node or canary token is an immediate, actionable incident.
